# Owner request: photograph the vehicle's factory colour / paint label (English)

> **A research template, not an official procedure.** It does **not ask for your VIN** and does **not assume** where on the vehicle a label sits: BYD's public documents do not currently say whether, or where, a given model carries a colour label. Taking part is entirely voluntary and you can stop at any time.

## What we would like to know

On **your own vehicle**, please check whether there is any **factory-applied or stamped plate/sticker** that shows **colour or paint information** (the field might be called "颜色", "车身颜色", "油漆", "涂装", or in English "Color", "Paint", "Paint code" ...).

- **If you find one:** photograph it following `evidence_photo_checklist.md` and tell us **where it is on the car** in your own words.
- **If you do not find one:** that is equally valuable - tell us **which places you checked**. **Do not remove any trim or part to look for it.**

## What you can tell us (all optional)

1. Brand, model, model year, powertrain (e.g. BYD Seal 2023 ...)
2. The **colour name** on your sales contract or brochure (e.g. 亚特兰蒂斯灰, 雪域白)
3. Whether the finish looks **gloss or matte** (if you know)
4. Whether the car has had **touch-up or respray work**
5. Photos of the label (following the checklist, with the **VIN and personal data covered**)
6. The letters/digits on the label **exactly as printed** (copy them before comparing with any code you saw online)

## Vehicle identity (optional)

To tie a photo reliably to a specific car, you may **voluntarily** choose one option (**never the full VIN**):

- Option A: give only the **first 9 characters** of the VIN (they identify the model, not your car);
- Option B: compute the SHA-256 hash of the full VIN **yourself, locally**, and send only the hash;
- Option C: give no VIN information (the evidence tier will be lower - a lead only).

We will **not** ask for, store or publish a full VIN.

## Privacy

- No name, address, number plate or phone number is needed; please cover them in photos.
- Please strip **GPS location metadata** from the photos.
- We keep only: model/year/colour name, the label's letters/digits, the label's field name, your description of its position, and the hash or 9-character prefix if you chose one.
- You may ask us to delete what you sent at any time.

## How a photo is used

- One label photo supports **that vehicle's** record only; it does **not** automatically change the code for other models or years.
- A record can become VERIFIED_LABEL only when the vehicle association, the label's authenticity and the meaning of the code field are sufficiently established.
- Otherwise it is kept as a lead. Codes are **never** decided by how many websites repeat them.
