## F. What cannot be stored (licensing / privacy)

- BASF Formula Finder formulas, toner lists, mixing ratios (property of BASF; see `batch8/baslac_manual_test_plan.md`).
- Full VINs, owner identities, photographs and screenshots of private vehicle records (store only SHA-256 + first 9 characters; originals stay in a private store).
- Anything obtained by accepting terms, logging in or paying on the researcher's own initiative.
